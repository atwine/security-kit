# Security policy

If you find a security hole in PROJECT NAME, please tell us privately
first, so we can fix it before anyone misuses it.

**How to report:** email CONTACT ADDRESS (an address the organisation
reads, not a personal one). Include what you found, how to reproduce it,
and what it lets someone do. Please don't include real people's data.

**What happens next:** we confirm we got your report within 5 working
days, keep you told of progress, and credit you when it's fixed, unless
you'd rather we didn't.

**Please don't:** access other people's data, change or delete data, or
disrupt the service while testing. Test only with accounts you created.

The same contact is published at `/.well-known/security.txt` on the site.
